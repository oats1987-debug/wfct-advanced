Raffles

How Prize Entries Work

Normal line raffles are tiered. Higher line totals enter every lower tier too, but each person can only win once. The drawings happen in order: 3 line raffle, then 2 line raffle, then 1 line raffle.

Power Up Raffle 1+ powered up line
2 winners: $25 Mog Station mount OR Genie Lamp mount.
3 Line Raffle 3+ normal lines
2 winners: 25 mil each. Also entered in the 2 line and 1 line raffles unless you win here.
2 Line Raffle 2+ normal lines
4 winners: 15 mil each. Also entered in the 1 line raffle unless you win here.
1 Line Raffle 1+ normal line
6 winners: 5 mil each.
Total Prize Pool Over 140 mil in gil prizes!

The Game

How WFCT Works

The Goal Each person gets a different card filled with FFXIV content. Team up with different participants to find matching content, complete squares, and build bingo lines.
Powered Squares Squares have upgraded versions that power them up. A full powered up line puts you into the enhanced powered up raffle.

The Book

Using Your Card

  1. Accept a book
  2. Enter your character name and a password you will remember. Use something easy that you do not use anywhere else.
  3. Your randomized bingo card will be filled with events.
  4. Do the content with another person who has the same content.
  5. Screenshot yourself completing it.
  6. Check the normal or power up box, depending on which version you did.
  7. Upload your screenshot proof.
  8. You can reset your entire card 1 time if you do not like your tiles. This resets everything and cannot be undone.
